Hello! This is our ReadMe. 
We learned here how to use Github, commit iterations of Arduino code, and push final versions when finished. 
We showed how to take an analog reading and convert it to digital output, noting the Arduino’s 10 bit resolution. We showed the ability to take many data points and average over them to get a higher resolution. We were finally able to wire up an LED to a potentiometer and change its apparent brightness by altering the duty cycle.
